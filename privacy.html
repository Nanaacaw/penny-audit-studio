Privacy Policy

Penny Audit Studio · last updated November 19, 2026

Penny Audit Studio is a private tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish their own videos. Only the owner signs in to it.

What it accesses, and why

  • YouTube (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl, youtube.readonly): it uploads the owner's videos to the owner's channels as Private, adds their thumbnails and captions, sets the time they go public, and reads view, like and comment counts for the owner's own report.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): it backs up the videos it made into a folder it created in the owner's Drive. It cannot see any other Drive file.

Where the data stays

Sign-in tokens and everything the tool reads are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to anyone else, sold, shared, or used for advertising. The tool has no server of its own.

Use of Google user data

Penny Audit Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the tool's Channel page, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it deletes the stored sign-in tokens.

Contact

Questions: through the The Penny Audit channel page, or the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
