Penny Audit Studio

Penny Audit Studio is a private tool its owner runs on their own computer to make short videos for two YouTube channels, The Penny Audit and TekaNigma.

With the owner's permission it uploads finished videos to their own YouTube channels as Private, sets thumbnails, captions and publish times, reads the channels' public statistics, and backs up the files it made to the owner's own Google Drive.

It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up.
